WHAT IKIMINA IS

A savings group older than any app

An ikimina is a small group of people neighbours, colleagues, relatives who agree to save a set amount together every month and lend from that pool to members who need it. It runs on trust, a shared notebook, and whoever in the group is careful with numbers.

The idea doesn't change here. What changes is who has to remember the arithmetic.

This system is that notebook, moved online for one specific group. It adds up what each person has saved, works out what they may borrow, and won't let a loan through until it has been approved the way your group agreed it should be by two different admins and a supervisor, in that order.

Nobody's balance depends on someone's memory of who paid in March. It's recorded once, and everyone with an account can see it.

Built for one groupThis ledger belongs to a specific ikimina and its members not a public marketplace.
Three rolesMembers see the book. Admins record payments and sign off loans. A supervisor gives the final word.
Nothing hiddenEvery payment, approval and repayment is on record, with who did it and when.
HOW IT WORKS

From monthly saving to a loan in your hand

The same four moves your group already makes on paper recorded automatically instead.

01

Save monthly

Each member pays their fixed amount in. It's logged against their name for that month, and the group total updates on its own.

02

Request a loan

A member can ask to borrow up to their total savings times the group's multiplier the ledger works out the limit and won't let a request pass it.

03

Get approved

Two different admins sign off, then the supervisor. Nobody approves their own request, and the order can't be skipped.

04

Repay, and share the earnings

The loan and its interest are due within the group's set window. Interest collected is shared back to members when the group decides to.

WHY IT HELPS

What the book couldn't do for you

⊞

Limits calculate themselves

A member's borrowing limit updates the moment their savings do no working it out by hand before a request.

✓

Approvals can't be rushed

A loan physically cannot reach "approved" without two separate admins and a supervisor, in order.

🕑

Due dates track themselves

The repayment window starts the day money actually leaves the box, not the day it was requested.

☰

One record, every device

Whoever opens the ledger on a phone or a laptop sees the same numbers, updated as they happen.

⋅

A paper trail for every shilling

Every payment and every approval is signed with a name and a date, kept for as long as the group runs.

★

Earnings, not just debts

Interest the group earns from loans is tracked separately and shared out the way members agree to.
